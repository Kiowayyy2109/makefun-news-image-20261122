The World in One Page — 22 November 2026: a program that draws the day's world news as a one-page newspaper front page. Lead story: the Gulf war widens as Iran hits a U.S. air base in Kuwait and targets Bahrain, a tanker burns in the Strait of Hormuz, and Trump says he wants a "permanent" deal to stop Iran ever getting a nuclear weapon. Also inside: the tech rout deepens as the Nasdaq falls 5.28% and Nvidia drops 14%, a magnitude 6.9 earthquake shakes Peru's Andes, Zelensky calls the Crimea bridge strikes a "fair response", FBI most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" is captured in Sinaloa, Bolsonaro is in intensive care, and COP31 climate talks deadlock over fossil fuels. Every story was reported 21–22 November 2026 (AP, Reuters, BBC, CNN, The Economist). The program draws the image procedurally with Pillow (bundled DejaVu fonts, no network, fully deterministic) and falls back to the bundled `artwork.png` when Pillow is unavailable.
